# Source-checked experimental model v5 — 2026-09-26

The real account remains paused. Model v5 changes no live budget or safety
control. It requires the primary rule's measurement/source/station to match the
recorded candidate, and Weather Company markets require same-station readings
from weather.com/kalshi. Each qualifying decision stores the primary rule,
source, station, observation window and raw readings, both forecast families'
remaining extrema and generation times, observed extreme, model probability and
uncertainty, executable price, contracts, fee, market gap and support check in
`decision_benchmarks.details_json`.

Forecast error is learned only from final Weather Company daily outcomes and
Open-Meteo forecasts ingested **before that station-day starts**. Each
station-day/extreme contributes once. The predeclared event split keeps holdout
days out of the live error estimate. At this review there were 32 development
station-days and 11 holdout station-days with complete pre-day forecasts. The
holdout mean absolute forecast error was 4.7 F. The development error produced
a conservative 10.675 F forecast standard deviation, above the 4 F fallback.
These statistics measure temperature error, not trading profit or calibrated
market probabilities.

As-of replay used each real order's durable `created_at`, only Weather Company
observations ingested by then, the latest Open-Meteo and NWS forecast runs
ingested/generated by then, remaining forecast points in the market's parsed
local-day window, and v5 uncertainty. It did not use later outcomes to predict
those orders. The results were:

| Market | Entry NO price | Old NO estimate | v5 NO estimate | v5 model confidence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Denver daily low 51-52 F | 6 cents | 95% | 43.502% | 0.0122 |
| Los Angeles daily low 68-69 F | 9 cents | 95% | 52.763% | 0.0022 |
| Chicago daily low 58-59 F | 58 cents | 95% | 61.223% | 0.0367 |

All three fail the unchanged 0.70 model-confidence gate. The old bug was daily
minimum parsed as maximum from secondary boilerplate; that parser was already
fixed in v4. The replay demonstrates v5 does not recreate the false 95% entries.

For new decisions a model/ask probability gap over 30 percentage points is
blocked unless both current forecast families agree within 2 F and predict the
proposed outcome. Normal pre-POST risk-limit rejections skip the order without
pausing; uncertain submissions and reconciliation errors remain blocking.

No v5 trade has a settled outcome yet, so v5 out-of-sample bet win rate and
fee-adjusted P&L are unavailable. The previous v4 incident review found only
six complete resolved development events, one hypothetical passing trade for
+$0.22 net, and zero usable resolved holdout events. That is not evidence of
positive expected return. Real historical result at this review: three trades,
