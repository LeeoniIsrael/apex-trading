# 2026-09-26 live weather probability incident

At investigation start, the exchange and exact-fill ledger reconciled to $92.3202
cash, $5.6883 open cost, and $0 realized P&L. Three NO positions remained open:
Denver 31 contracts at 6 cents plus $0.1224 fee ($1.9824 total); Los Angeles
20 at 9 cents plus $0.1147 fee ($1.9147); Chicago 3 contracts ($1.7912 total).
New orders were paused in both databases before diagnosis. These are unsettled
positions, not realized losses or wins.

## Cause and saved evidence

The individual Kalshi primary rules say **minimum** temperature at CLIDEN,
CLILAX, and CLIMDW for September 25, 2026, using The Weather Company. Each is
an inclusive two-degree range. Their generic secondary rules mention
"maximum/minimum". The old parser searched combined rule text for maximum
before minimum and classified all three as daily highs. It stored a 73 F high
for Denver and 77 F for Los Angeles at the trade decisions, yielding 5% YES
and 95% NO. This was a model-input interpretation error, not a reversed price
side: NO asks came from 100 minus the YES bid (94 -> 6 cents in Denver; 91 ->
9 cents in Los Angeles). Fill fees and cash matched the exchange. Denver's
local-day UTC window was 06:00 to 06:00 and Los Angeles's was 07:00 to 07:00;
those station, date, and DST mappings were correct. Official Weather Company
daily results still reported `no_report` during the investigation.

Replaying only observations and forecast runs ingested by each fill timestamp
with the corrected parser gives modeled NO probabilities of 0.36365 (Denver),
0.38399 (Los Angeles), and 0.59495 (Chicago). These are uncertain forecasts,
not calibrated probabilities. The saved preliminary lows were 51.1 F, 68.0 F,
and 60.1 F respectively. Later quotes cannot retroactively establish the
probability at fill time; they can reveal model disagreement. Current NO
bid/ask at the investigation was 1/2 cents for Denver, 8/12 for Los Angeles,
and 70/87 for Chicago. Quotes are not outcomes.

The repair derives measurement and threshold only from the primary rule,
rejects conflicting extrema in that rule, and handles the live `between 51-52`
range syntax. A new model version prevents invalid old candidates from being
authorized by the live executor. A paused worker can start for read-only
reconciliation; each submission still checks the full pause and safety gate.

## Evaluation limits

An as-of replay used the first old qualifying candidate per resolved event,
the saved executable orderbook ask and depth, the existing $2 budget and gates,
and quadratic fees. This is a narrow counterfactual because markets were
selected under the faulty model. Six resolved events had complete as-of data;
all six were in the development split. The corrected forecast Brier score was
0.268 across those six. One hypothetical trade passed unchanged gates:
modeled fee-inclusive EV +$0.1148 and settled hypothetical P&L +$0.22. Five
were skipped; the observed sample has no settled trade loss. Neither this
single trade nor the old model's trades prove positive expected value.

There were zero resolved usable holdout events. Of the three fresh incident
markets, Chicago was preassigned to holdout and remained unsettled. The
corrected probabilities fail the existing model-confidence gate for these
three entries; no new signal or threshold was adopted. A calibrated
out-of-sample EV and loss rate are unavailable. Real positions carry up to
$5.6883 in current open cost, and settlement could lose it all.
