# $100 preparation: evidence, costs, and current limits

No live enablement occurred. No recent source establishes a dependable, unpatched
weather-market profit opportunity. Social-media P&L screenshots are not used as
calibration evidence or grounds for removing safety limits.

## Primary-source findings

- [Kalshi weather rules](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823837-weather-markets),
  updated July 22, 2026: the named source and reporting window are authoritative;
  preliminary readings and rounding can differ from final settlement. A observed
  breach is a research hypothesis, not a guaranteed payout.
- [July 7 fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf): taker and
  some maker fees vary by multiplier. Cheap contracts and passive orders are not
  automatically profitable. Paper cent rounding remains conservative;
  [live fill fee components](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/fee_rounding)
  require exact reconciliation before activation.
- [Current order API](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/orders/create-order-v2)
  quotes the YES leg, uses decimal strings, and supports immediate-or-cancel.
  The prepared adapter now maps NO correctly, prevents self-trades and requests
  cancel-on-pause. Real trading stays disabled pending end-to-end recovery.
- [OpenAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing): GPT-6 Luna
  standard input/output prices verified at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens.
  Chat routes unfamiliar wording to fixed, database-grounded explanations.

## Implemented prospective experiment

`two-sided-budget-v2` evaluates YES and NO at executable top-of-book prices,
reserves fees inside a $2 entry budget, and ranks after-fee expected profit.
Minimum edge is now truly after fees. Minimum liquidity measures quoted depth,
not how many contracts a small account can afford. The deterministic probability
engine is unchanged; its model uncertainty still requires empirical calibration.

Portfolio-independent shadow signals continue while trading is blocked. They are
not fills or realized profits. Stale data, uncertain rules, confidence, spread and
net-edge checks still apply to shadow signals; risk controls still govern orders.
The fixed v2 hash partitions entire station/date groups (all sources together),
with 20% holdout and an independently assigned 10% no-trade control. Old v1 results
are not relabeled. Each arm uses its first qualifying observation per ticker;
readiness uses one per event. Arms share outcomes and are not independent tests.

Priority hypotheses: fresh official-observation repricing lag; near-close residual
uncertainty; separate station/source calibration; better-priced minority outcomes.
These hypotheses have no proven positive out-of-sample results yet. Maker quoting,
multi-leg arbitrage and higher leverage are not deployed: queue position, leg risk
and execution failures need separate validation, especially with a $100 budget.

## Running cost policy

Routine research and weather probability use no LLM calls. Jev and runtime review
remain optional and are disabled in the deployed low-cost profile until their
incremental value is measured. Chat is capped at $0.03/day and $0.50/calendar month,
counts recorded AI costs together, reserves budget atomically, and caches only
the selected topic for five minutes (never stale balances). Common questions use
no API call. Usage uncertainty retains the reservation. These are application
API caps; this development conversation uses the operator's separate Codex plan.

The configured VPS cost is $21.09/month, separate from the AI cap. It materially
burdens a $100 account. No cheaper server was purchased or account resized here.
The 22.33% historical drawdown includes operating costs charged against the initial
$100. Costs remain visible; no history is erased and no threshold is loosened.

## Before risking money

The live funding ceiling is $100; a larger connected balance is rejected until
funds are isolated. Entry limit $2 including estimated fees; maximum exposure $10;
city exposure $5; daily loss stop $2; drawdown stop 10%; daily orders 10. Profits
that take the account above $100 also require operator review under this conservative
funding check. This is not yet an unattended live trading product.

Still required: prospective independent positive results, readiness thresholds,
authenticated account/balance verification, complete remote fill/position recovery,
exact live fees including event overrides, and deployment-specific emergency-stop
and alert checks. No evidence records may be invented to pass readiness. Only a
separate explicit operator activation may create a live marker and change mode.
